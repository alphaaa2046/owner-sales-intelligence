About this prototype

This is a static mockup of the Rep Prep Companion. In production:

  • Restaurant data auto-populates from PCR (Salesloft side panel)
  • Suggested openers, questions, and friction handlers generate dynamically based on patterns from continuous call scoring
  • This functionality lives inside PCR rather than as a standalone tool
  • Rep feedback (👍 / 👎) flows back into prompt refinement and dashboard signal

The 10 restaurants here are illustrative. Production runs across the full call queue with content tuned per call type, segment, and individual rep.
